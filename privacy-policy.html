Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 • Effective immediately

Summary: At Jigsaw Masters, we believe in providing a relaxing, family-friendly gaming experience. We respect your personal privacy and only collect minimal data necessary to maintain your game saves, sync progress, and ensure app stability.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Jigsaw Masters ("we", "us", "our", or the "Game"), available on iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store). It governs how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you play our game or interact with our website.

By installing, accessing, or playing Jigsaw Masters, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue using the Game.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data required to provide you with seamless puzzle gameplay:

A. Gameplay & Progress Information

  • Player Progress: Completed puzzle levels, streak counts, daily puzzle completion records, star ratings, and unlock status for Mystery Vault and VIP Artworks.
  • In-Game Currency: Your current balance of earned or purchased virtual coins.
  • Game Settings: Preferences such as sound volume, music toggles, and notification settings.

B. Device & Technical Identifiers

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), screen resolution, device language, and system performance metrics.
  • Anonymous Identifier: A randomly generated pseudonymous user identifier (UUID) used to synchronize and restore your puzzle saves.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Logs: Anonymous stack traces and crash logs to help our development team identify and resolve technical glitches.

C. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases (such as coin packs or VIP access) are processed exclusively by Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play Store). We never collect, store, or have access to your credit card, debit card, or financial billing details. We only receive encrypted transaction tokens to credit your in-game coins.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Providing, operating, and preserving your game progress across app launches.
  • Enabling streak tracking, daily coin rewards, and mystery vault unlocks.
  • Troubleshooting crashes, improving game stability, and optimizing loading times.
  • Complying with legal obligations and platform requirements (Apple & Google).

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To maintain Jigsaw Masters as a free-to-play title, we may display third-party advertisements or integrate diagnostic SDKs. These trusted partners may process non-sensitive device identifiers under their respective privacy policies:

  • Apple Game Center / Google Play Games: For cloud achievements, backup sync, and leaderboards.
  • Google AdMob & Unity Ads: To serve optional rewarded video ads (e.g. for bonus hints or coins).
  • Firebase / Crashlytics: For crash reporting and anonymous telemetry.

You can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising anytime in your device system settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > toggle off tracking requests.
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Jigsaw Masters is a relaxing, family-safe puzzle game designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or retain personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 where required by local law). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption and secure cloud key vaults, to protect your gameplay data. We retain gameplay progress data for as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the data associated with your player profile.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion (Right to be Forgotten): Permanently delete your player profile, cloud backup, and progress data.
Need to delete your game data?
You can delete your account and all associated gameplay history anytime directly in-app or via our web form on our Account & Data Deletion Page.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact our support team:

  • Email: support@jigsawmastersgame.com
  • Game: Jigsaw Masters Support Team
  • Account Deletion: jigsawmastersgame.com/delete-account
