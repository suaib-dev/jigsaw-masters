Account & Data Deletion

Manage your account deletion and data removal requests for Jigsaw Masters

Important Notice: Deleting your account will permanently erase all your game progress, earned and purchased coins, unlocked VIP gallery masterpieces, mystery vault solutions, and streak history. This action is irreversible.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

In accordance with Apple App Store and Google Play Data Safety policies, upon confirmed request for account deletion, we permanently remove or anonymize:

  • Player Profile & User ID: Your unique anonymous game account and linked authentication credentials.
  • Puzzle Progress & Solved Levels: All level completion states across all categories.
  • Coin Balance: All accumulated virtual currency and daily reward bonuses.
  • Unlocked Collections: Access to unlocked VIP Masterpieces (e.g. Brooklyn Bridge, Cinque Terre) and solved Mystery Vault enigmas.
  • Daily Streaks: Streak counters, calendar badges, and personal records.
  • Cloud Backups: Any associated cloud save data stored on our servers.

Choose Your Preferred Deletion Method

Method 1 • Recommended

Delete Directly In-App (Instant)

If you currently have Jigsaw Masters installed on your phone or tablet:

  1. Open Jigsaw Masters on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings (⚙️) gear icon in the top right corner.
  3. Select Account & Data Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Erase Data.
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your data will be deleted immediately.
Method 2 • Web Request

Submit Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device:

Fill out the verified request form below. Our support team will process your request and permanently remove your game records within 7 to 14 business days.

Online Account Deletion Request Form

Please provide your details so we can locate and securely purge your account records from our servers.

You can find your Player ID at the bottom of the in-game Settings screen.

Frequently Asked Questions About Deletion

How long does the web deletion process take?

In-app deletion is immediate. Web requests are verified and permanently purged from active and backup databases within 7 to 14 business days.

Can I get a refund for unspent coins?

In accordance with standard Apple and Google app store terms, unspent in-game virtual currencies cannot be refunded or converted to cash upon account deletion.

What should I do if I have an active subscription?

Deleting your game account does not automatically cancel third-party app store subscriptions. You must manage and cancel active subscriptions directly through your device account settings:

  • Apple (iOS): Open Settings > tap your Apple ID Name > Subscriptions > Jigsaw Masters > Cancel Subscription.
  • Google (Android): Open Google Play Store > Profile icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions > Cancel.

Need help or have questions?

If you have questions about your data or encounter any difficulty during the deletion process, please email our support team directly at support@jigsawmastersgame.com.
